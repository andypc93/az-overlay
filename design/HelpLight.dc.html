Quick start
01
Pick your layout
Choose + New layout and start with a template for your device.
02
Find the perfect spot
On Layout, choose Edit on screen. Drag to move, scroll to resize, then choose Done editing.
03
Connect your inputs
On Keys, select a pad and choose Record input to assign a key or controller button.
04
Make it yours
Adjust fonts and colors on Appearance. Changes to saved layouts are saved automatically.
